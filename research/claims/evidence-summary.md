# Frozen Evidence Summary

## E1 and E2

The early pilot found correctness ceiling effects and a preliminary efficiency signal. The harder v2 run exposed budget censoring, redundant native reconstruction, a three-valued semantic failure, and scorer defects. The four-slot v2.1 calibration then implemented interface-first access. Held-out paired replication reproduced the efficiency direction in all 10 pairs while both arms passed the real runtime check.

The held-out report records all three primary efficiency endpoints in the same direction for 10 of 10 pairs and reports Holm-adjusted exact sign-test values of `0.0029296875`. Preserve the report as the authority for definitions, exclusions, and statistics.

## Runtime correctness

The referenced held-out runs observed no runtime correctness loss. Their frozen overall primary result was affected by an ID-versus-label scorer defect. The later contract-aware adjudication is an audit, not a replacement for the frozen score.

## E3

E3 v0.1 found a Soft ceiling and one treatment budget exhaustion. E3 v0.2 removed the cue and budget confounds; both arms remained correct. The depth experiment extended local transformation depth through Tier 3 and recorded a bounded Soft ceiling: 12 of 12 valid runs, with no false-certainty contrast. The checker correctly rejects typed-obligation violations and remains a mechanical assurance mechanism.

## E4

The dependency-depth study ran both arms over eight depths. All 16 trajectories completed and passed the real runtime check. The frozen primary score was 6/8 Native and 8/8 Semantic, but the two Native losses were evidence/provenance closure failures with correct facts, paths, repair, and runtime behavior. The evidence supports lower reconstruction cost; it does not establish a correctness capability-boundary shift.

## Product interpretation

The current evidence supports a formally grounded, auditable runtime interface that removes a replicated software-state reconstruction tax for strong Agents in the tested task family. Broader correctness, capability, task, and model claims remain hypotheses.
